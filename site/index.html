Up and running in a minute

No installer and no Python. Just unzip it and go.

  1. 1

    Download and unzip

    Put the folder anywhere you like: Documents, another drive, a USB stick.

  2. 2

    Pick a model

    Choose one in the Model dropdown and download it from the Models page.

  3. 3

    Choose what to listen to

    Microphone, system audio, or just the apps you tick. Ticked apps are picked up whenever they start.

  4. 4

    Press Start

    Captions appear as people talk. Turn on the overlay to keep them on top.

Which model should I use?

Every model runs on your PC. The app warns you when one will probably be too slow for real time.

Whisper large-v3-turbo

GPU

The default, for an NVIDIA GPU with 6 GB or more

Close to large-v3 accuracy and much faster: partial captions in about 0.3 s on an RTX 4070 Laptop. 100 languages.

Breeze ASR 25

GPU

Have an NVIDIA GPU?

Open the Models page and click Download on the NVIDIA GPU support card (about 1 GB, once). A model that's already loaded moves to the GPU as soon as it finishes. Only the display driver is needed; you don't have to install the CUDA Toolkit.

Privacy

Your audio stays on your PC

Speech is recognized locally and never uploaded. The app goes online only when you ask it to:

  • Downloading a model from Hugging Face
  • Downloading GPU support from PyPI
  • Checking GitHub for a new version

Everything else, from starting the app to captioning, works without internet.

Questions and answers

Which versions of Windows does it work on?

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Captioning individual apps needs Windows build 20348 or later (in practice Windows 11); on older versions the microphone and system audio still work.

Do I need a graphics card?

No. Without an NVIDIA GPU, models run on the CPU; pick Whisper base or small for real time. With an NVIDIA GPU and a recent driver, download GPU support on the Models page and use the bigger models.

Does it need the internet?

Only to download models and GPU support, and when you check for updates. After that it works completely offline.

Where does it keep my settings and transcripts?

Next to AppLiveCaption.exe: settings in config.json, transcripts in history, plus the logs, models and GPU support folders. Nothing goes to AppData, so moving or deleting the folder moves or removes everything. (In a folder Windows won't let it write to, such as C:\Program Files, it uses %LOCALAPPDATA%\AppLiveCaption instead.)

Can it caption just one app, like a browser or Discord?

Yes. Choose Apps as the source and tick the apps you want; only their audio is used, optionally mixed with your microphone.

Does it translate?

No. It writes down what is said, in the language it's said in. Translation and speaker identification are out of scope.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. AppLiveCaption is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license. The speech recognition models are made by their authors and come with their own licenses, shown on the Models page.

Is it made with AI?

Yes, it's vibe-coded! I built it with Claude Code, Anthropic's AI coding assistant, running Claude Opus 5.5. Without this amazing tool, I wouldn't have been able to build the app I wanted in such a short time.
